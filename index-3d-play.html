FAQ

Common questions

What is LINKAR?

LINKAR is your trusted link to car care: a platform connecting car owners with certified service centers, so you can compare, book and pay in one app.

Which services are available at launch?

We start with window film, paint protection film (PPF) and ceramic coating, then gradually add washing, polishing, maintenance, tires, batteries and more.

When do you launch?

We're putting on the finishing touches. Register your interest and we'll notify you at launch with your exclusive discount.

I own a service center. How do I join?

Choose "Service provider" in the form, enter your company name and contact details, and the LINKAR team will contact you to complete certification.

Does LINKAR serve companies and fleets?

Yes. LINKAR is built for individuals, companies and fleets. Register your interest and we'll get in touch with details.
